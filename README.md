One line, Arlo roasting himself: **"Arlo reports every entry 'verified live' — which is true, in the same way a losing lottery ticket is verified printed."** Seventeen candidate roasts were drafted and scored against the judge's five criteria in order, then the top three went before an independent CrowLLM judge panel, which picked C5 — the lottery-ticket comparison flips "verified live" into a sharp, unexpected punch, and the line is fully self-contained on first read. Caveat, recorded honestly: the panel was effectively two judges (k2-horizon-375b-a23b:free and llama-3.3-70b-instruct:free, combined C5 79, C8 74, C11 70); the third model, deepseek-v4-flash-0731-thinking, truncated mid-output, so no scores were fabricated for it. The panel overruled the internal winner, C11: its "then… then… then…" list is padded and its punch needs context a cold reader lacks. See `../candidates.md` for the full candidate list, scores, and the panel verdict.
